How to get affordable International flights?

Getting your hands on the most affordable international flights is easy if you know what to do. You just need a few right tips and voila! Great deals are on their way to save big for your next trip.

Here are some smart hacks to help you book smarter and cheaper:

  • Flexibility with dates: If your schedule allows, fly midweek, often considered a great time for lower airfares due to low demand.
  • Book in advance: Most airlines you’ll see offer better prices if you book and plan your trip in advance.
  • Use flight alerts: Set up fare alerts to know about the deals we are offering.
  • Avoid peak season travel: It'll be a lot better if you can travel during the shoulder seasons. They have less demand and come at better fares.
  • Pick a nearby airport: Flying out of airports near your city tends to be a lot cheaper.
  • Check for group fares: Traveling with family or friends can get you a great group flight discount, helping you save significantly.

Factors to consider for International flights booking

For traveling across borders, you need to be smart when planning your trip. You need to make an informed choice, keeping a lot of factors in mind, like baggage rules, travel insurance, visa, seating, and more. To help you with smooth flight booking, we’ve made a list of top factors you need to consider for your next global adventure:

  • Flight duration and layovers: Minimum layover flights are the best if you don’t want any stress and want to save your time as well.
  • Visa and entry requirements: Before finalizing anything, check the visa rules of your destination.
  • Baggage allowance: Only start packing after you’re familiar with all the baggage rules for your airline.
  • Travel insurance: You may have to cancel last minute, or there may be delays or medical emergencies. For those times, travel insurance is important.
  • Flexible booking options: Always pick flights that come with easy changes in case your plan changes at the last second.
  • Airline reputation: We’ve got only reputable airlines here at Infinity Travels to help you fly in comfort and with great customer service.
