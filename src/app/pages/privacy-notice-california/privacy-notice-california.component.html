This notice is for residents of California and describes the practices of Infinity Travels under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). This privacy notice aligns with our main Privacy Policy available on our website. In this document, we detail how we gather, handle, disclose, and protect the personal information of our customers from California.

How We Use Your Personal Data

Here at Infinity Travels, we collect your personal data for important purposes, which include:

  • Managing and completing your travel bookings
  • Offering your assistance through our reliable team and addressing your inquiries
  • Identifying and stopping any fraudulent activities
  • Meeting our required legal responsibilities
  • Improve the quality of what we offer and optimize our website’s functionality

Who We Share Information With

Occasionally, Infinity Travels discloses the personal information of its California customers to trusted third parties, such as:

  • Travel partners – airlines, hotels, and service providers who need to complete your travel arrangements
  • Secure payment gateways and fraud detection systems
  • Support tools, along with partners who focus on marketing and data analysis
  • Official authorities only as required under regulations

Your Rights Under CCPA and CPRA

As a California resident, you may enjoy certain protections, allowing you to:

  • Get details on your personal data we maintain
  • Ask us to give you updates on any incorrect details in your records
  • Tell us to delete your personal data (comes with certain limitations)
  • Choose to restrict the sale or sharing of your personal data
  • Access the same level of service and rates, irrespective of using these options
