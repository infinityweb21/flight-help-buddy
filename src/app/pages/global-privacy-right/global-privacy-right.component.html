Global Privacy Rights of Infinity Travels (California)

At Infinity Web Solutions LLC (“Infinity Travels,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), our users' privacy is our top priority. This page explains the privacy rights of California residents, along with the European Union (EU), the United Kingdom (UK), and other jurisdictions with similar data privacy laws.

This Global Privacy Rights Notice explains our position on the Privacy Policy. In case of conflicts between Global Privacy Rights and Privacy Policy, note that this policy only applies to residents of the aforesaid jurisdictions.

Users Privacy Rights

You may benefit from some or all of the following rights depending on your location:

  • Right to Access (Right to Know)
    California (CCPA): You can ask us to share either categories or specific pieces of your personal information we have collected in the past 12 months.
    EU/UK (GDPR): You can ask us to share a copy of your stored personal information.
  • Right to Correct (Rectification): Corrections to incorrect or incomplete personal information are available to you.
  • Right to Delete (Eradicate):
    CCPA (California): Request deletion of personal information we hold, subject to judicial terms.
    GDPR (EU/UK): Request the complete deletion of stored personal data if it’s no longer necessary.
  • Right to Limit or Object to:
    GDPR (EU/UK): You hold the right to limit the use of your personal data for marketing or restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request a copy of your shared personal data in a structured and common format.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing of Data:
    CCPA (California): Infinity Travels complies with California Law (CCPA). Hence, we will never sell your personal data under any circumstances.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent:
    GDPR (EU/UK): You can withdraw your consent at any time, and we guarantee that we will no longer use this data in the future.

How to Exercise Rights

To exercise any of their data protection rights, users have the following options:

  • For GDPR-related requests, contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO) at booking@infinitytravels.com.

We will respond to each request within the designated timeframe as per the applicable law. You may need to verify your identity before initiating any actions in response to your request.

Authorized Agents

Residents of California may hire an authorized agent to assist them in submitting a privacy request on their behalf. However, to do so, they must provide written authorization to verify the identity of both the user and the agent.

Non-Discrimination

We, at Infinity Travels, pledge to treat everyone equally, without imposing any discrimination. We’ll never treat you unfairly, deny services, charge different prices, or give substandard service for exercising your privacy rights.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed within the United States of America due to legal variations. Privacy laws may be different from those in your country. However, we take necessary measures to safeguard (such as encryption and standard contractual clauses) your information for such transfers.
