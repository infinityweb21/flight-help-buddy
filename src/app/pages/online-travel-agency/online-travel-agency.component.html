Travel Documents You Cannot Afford to Miss

Preparing your travel documents in advance can help make your airport experience more convenient. Before heading to the airport, make sure you have the essential documents required for your journey:

  • Valid passport.
  • Visa or approved ESTA authorization, when required.
  • Confirmed flight reservation.
  • Government-issued photo identification.

Depending on your destination and travel circumstances, additional documents may be necessary. Check the latest airline and destination entry requirements before departure to ensure your travel documents are ready.
