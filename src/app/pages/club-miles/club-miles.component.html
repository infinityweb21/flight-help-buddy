ClubMiles is {{companyname.companyName}}’s exclusive rewards program that allows members to earn points on all bookings. These miles can be used to get discounts, upgrades, and special offers. It’s designed to make your travel experiences more enjoyable and affordable by accumulating rewards with every trip, encouraging loyalty and rewarding frequent travelers.

You automatically earn ClubMiles when you book with {{companyname.companyName}}. Make sure you are logged into your account before completing your booking. Miles are credited after your trip is completed, based on the total amount spent, with additional bonuses during promotions or partner bookings, helping you accumulate rewards faster.

Generally, you earn 1 mile per dollar spent on your booking. However, promotional offers, special campaigns, or partner bookings may provide bonus miles. The exact miles earned depend on the booking type, amount, and ongoing promotions, so check your booking details or account to confirm your earnings.

Redeeming miles is straightforward. You can use your accumulated miles to get discounts, upgrades, or access exclusive offers either during the booking process or through your online rewards account. Ensure you have enough miles for redemption and follow the prompts to apply your rewards before confirming your trip.
