What Documents Are Required For International Travel?

When you are traveling internationally, there are some primary documents that you need to keep handy before starting your trip. The documents include:

  • Flight ticket
  • A valid passport
  • Visa for the destination country
  • Proof of accommodation
  • Travel insurance
  • Health certificates
