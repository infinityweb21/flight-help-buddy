Hong Kong: The Pearl of the Orient in Motion

The destination has emerged as one of the world’s most vibrant and dynamic cities, blending Eastern traditions with Western modernity. If you haven’t had the chance to explore this yet, then you’re truly missing out! Take a trip to this island city where skyscrapers meet mountains, and crowded markets coexist with peaceful temples.

Wander through the neon-lit streets of Mong Kok, take a ride on the Star Ferry, shop at the famous Temple Street Night Market, or enjoy panoramic views from Victoria Peak. Hong Kong is a place where you can savor authentic dim sum in the morning and visit world-class museums by afternoon. From the harbor to serene parks, this destination offers a unique experience at every turn.

Victoria Peak

Enjoy panoramic views of the city’s iconic skyline and surrounding islands from the highest point on Hong Kong Island to explore the Sky Terrace and savor fine dining with a view. It’s a must-visit location that perfectly captures the city's dynamic beauty.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Star Ferry Ride

Enjoy the legendary Star Ferry and cruise across Victoria Harbour to see the stunning contrast of old-world charm and futuristic skyscrapers. It's a quick and budget-friendly way to experience the city’s magic, especially during sunset or the nightly Symphony of Lights show.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Temple Street Night Market

Check out the crowded street life at Temple Street Night Market from street food stalls and bargain clothing to fortune-tellers and live music. It's the perfect place for late-night shopping and soaking in the city’s local vibe.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Big Buddha & Ngong Ping 360

Take a scenic cable car ride with views of the mountains and sea to visit the majestic Tian Tan Buddha (Big Buddha) on Lantau Island. This spiritual landmark is paired with the Ngong Ping Village, offering a peaceful escape and a taste of traditional culture and architecture.

Last Update: 20/07/2025
