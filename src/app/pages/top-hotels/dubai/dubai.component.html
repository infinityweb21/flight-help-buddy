The City that Rises from the Sands: Welcome to Dubai

The city has become the shining gem of the Middle East, renowned for its futuristic skyline, luxury lifestyle, and cultural richness. If this glamorous destination isn’t already on your travel list, you’re missing the opportunity to see a place where tradition and innovation coexist in perfect harmony. Get ready to explore this city of wonders.

Walk along the Dubai Marina, shop at the world-famous Dubai Mall, see the Burj Khalifa, or relax at the beautiful Jumeirah Beach. A city including the tallest building to the largest indoor aquarium where you can enjoy desert safaris or luxury in seven-star hotels, the city has something for everyone and don't forget to explore the old souks and museums that echo the city's rich heritage.

Burj Khalifa

Check out the tallest building in the world and see marvelous views from the observation decks offering a spectacular perspective and stand as a shining symbol of the city’s innovation.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Desert Safari Adventure

Escape the city and journey into the golden dunes of the Arabian Desert for a memorable adventure where you can enjoy dune bashing, camel rides, sandboarding, and a traditional camp to experience with BBQ dinner and henna painting.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Dubai Mall & Fountain Show

Shop at one of the world’s largest malls featuring luxury brands, an indoor aquarium, and entertainment. Don’t miss the spectacular Dubai Fountain show, a water and light performance set to music, taking place every evening by the Burj Khalifa lake.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Old Dubai & Gold Souk

Explore the historical side of the city in Al Fahidi Historical District and sail the Dubai Creek on a traditional abra boat. Then head to the Gold Souk, where displays of gold, spices, and perfumes offer a glimpse into the city’s trading heritage.

Last Update: 20/07/2025
