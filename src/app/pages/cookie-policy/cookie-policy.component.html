Cookies Policy

Let’s Know More About Cookies

Curious about the cookies that’re used on our websites? Tiny files known as cookies automatically get stored on your device the moment you visit our website. The goal of these files is to make it easier for our website to remember every tiny detail of your visit.

Wondering How We Use Cookies?

We generally use cookies so that it becomes easy for us to remember your preferences and improve the performance of the website. Many factors can impact a website, such as website traffic and visitor behavior. Cookies help us to understand these criteria so that we can improve our services and your website experience. We also want to make offers and recommendations that will suit your preferences. Cookies help us do that.

Know Our Third-Party Services

We also work with a team of trusted third-party partners. They also bring their cookies to work for the site. These cookies help us with advertising, monitoring performance, analyzing the website, and even marketing.

Which Cookies Stay And Expire?

You will get different types of cookies; some stay, and others expire. The persistent cookies will remain on your device for a particular period of time. With the help of these cookies, our site will remember your choices when you come back. But the other cookies will disappear as soon as you close your browser.

Manage Your Own Cookies

Manage your cookies however you want! If you want, you can easily block your cookies. You also have the power to change the settings or delete any existing cookies. But you must know that by disabling cookies, you will affect some of the features and functions of the website. And we’re not responsible for it.

Changes in the Policy

You’ll always know if there are any changes to our website or services. You can see the new versions on the cookies policy page of our website. We suggest you check occasionally.
