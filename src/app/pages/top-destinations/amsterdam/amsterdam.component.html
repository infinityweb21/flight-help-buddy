Amsterdam: The Soul of the Netherlands

The capital of the Dutch is located along a vast network of canals with history and modern life blend effortlessly. As you walk through cobblestone streets framed by charming 17th-century houses, the city opens up with vibrant neighborhoods, world-famous museums and a café culture. The city is more than tulips and bicycles with various ideas and art tells a story.

You can start your day with a buttery stroopwafel by the canal, see Van Gogh's masterpieces before noon and enjoy Indonesian rijsttafel for dinner. The city invites you to slow down and feel in its charm whether you’re on foot, by bike, or gliding along the canals. The Dutch capital never loses its cool even in the chillier months when fairy lights reflect off the frozen canals creating a beautiful winter scene.

Best Time To Visit New York City, USA

Spring is the most celebrated time in Amsterdam, with tulip fields in full bloom and mild weather perfect for canal cruises and cycling. The Keukenhof Gardens and King’s Day festivities make this the busiest and most colorful season for travelers.

Summer brings long daylight hours, lively street festivals, and open-air events. The weather is warm but not extreme, making it pleasant for outdoor exploration. Crowds are steady but manageable compared to spring, offering a good balance between activities and atmosphere.

Autumn and winter are quieter, with fewer tourists and lower accommodation costs. While colder months bring grey skies and occasional rain, they also offer cozy canal-side cafés, cultural museums, and the festive charm of Amsterdam’s Christmas markets and light festivals.

Rijksmuseum

A home to masterpieces from Rembrandt, Vermeer and many other painters. The building and gardens themselves are worth exploring beyond its stunning galleries which is perfect for travelers wanting to know about the Golden Age of Dutch art.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Canal Cruise

A unique way to see the city’s iconic architecture and bridges is by boat whether you take a romantic evening cruise or a historical daytime tour and floating through Amsterdam’s UNESCO-listed canal ring offers a fresh perspective on its charm.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Anne Frank House

Check out the hidden place where Anne Frank wrote her diary and this educational experience brings the realities of World War II to life and it’s best to book tickets in advance due to its importance.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Jordaan District

It is now a trendy neighborhood filled with independent boutiques, art galleries and some of the coziest cafés in the city. A morning spent wandering here is a mix of authenticity and local flavor.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Vondelpark

The most famous park is an escape right in the heart of the city with open lawns, small lakes and bike paths. It’s ideal for a picnic or simply watching the world go by and attend a free concert at the open-air theatre during summer.

Last Update: 20/07/2025

Heineken Experience

A former brewery turned interactive museum offering a fun look into one of the world's most iconic beer brands. It’s a great mix of entertainment and tradition from brewing history to a rooftop bar with city views.
