11. Cookie Policy

a. What Are Cookies?

Small files stored on your device while using a platform are cookies, and they help us to identify you, remember your interests, and analyze your patterns to give you a better user experience.

b. Cookie Types and Their Purposes
Type Purpose Examples
Essential Cookies To enable core website functions, such as hotel bookings and secure logins. Session cookies, checkout process cookies
Performance & Analytics Cookies To analyze visitor interactions with our site and enhance its functionality. Google Analytics, Hotjar
Advertising & Targeting Cookies To provide tailored ads and measure the effectiveness of our ad campaigns. Google Ads, Meta (Facebook) Pixel
Functionality Cookies To store your preferences, including language selections and recent search history. Preferred currency, destination filters
