Additional Terms & Conditions

Taxes & Pricing: Government taxes and fees may vary, but remember you only need to pay what is displayed on your final total at checkout. 

Refund Eligibility: Most tickets are non-refundable; an individual can only receive a refund if the airline allows it. 

Airline Control: All the decisions related to cash refunds and future travel credits are determined by the airlines based on certain policies.

Additional Service Fees: Processing fees are charged by {{companyname.companyName}} on any penalties or fees that are set by suppliers or the airlines.

Special Service Requests: Airline approval is mandatory for any requests for medical, military, or bereavement waivers. A service fee is charged only if the special request is approved and fulfilled.

Compassion Exception Policy

Eligible customers can receive reduced rates through our Compassion Exception Policy. The following categories are eligible:

  • Individuals traveling due to bereavement
  • Guests with visual impairments
  • Active and veteran military personnel
